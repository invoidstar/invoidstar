### Hello, I'm Invoidstar.

I'm **Qi Chu (初琦)**, a Ph.D. student at the **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)**. My research focuses on **embodied intelligence**, **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** models, and **robot learning**.

I'm interested in connecting multimodal understanding with grounded robot actions, and in building research tools that make experimentation more reproducible and accessible.

## 01 / Research Interests

**Vision-Language-Action Learning**  
Learning and adapting robot policies that connect visual perception, language goals, and actions. In particular, I explore temporal memory, task-stage understanding, and grounded action representations.

**Action-Conditioned World Modeling**  
Understanding and predicting how actions change the environment, from robot states to visual observations. I study how state, vision, language, and action can support better representations of world dynamics.

**Robust & Reproducible Robot Learning**  
Investigating VLA reliability under visual and semantic disturbances, alongside reproducible training and evaluation across robot tasks, datasets, and benchmarks.
